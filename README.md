# StuScout

## Overview

StuScout is an AI-powered student opportunity discovery platform that helps students find authentic hackathons, internships, scholarships, workshops, research programs, and career opportunities.

The platform combines institutional verification and artificial intelligence to ensure that students receive trustworthy, relevant, and personalized opportunities while minimizing exposure to fraudulent or outdated listings.

---

## Problem Statement

Students often rely on multiple sources such as social media, messaging groups, emails, and websites to find opportunities. These sources frequently contain:

- Duplicate opportunities
- Expired events
- Fake or suspicious listings
- Irrelevant recommendations
- Lack of verification

StuScout solves these challenges through a trust-layered verification framework and AI-powered recommendation engine.

## System Architecture

StuScout consists of five major layers:

1. User Layer
   - Students
   - Faculty Coordinators
   - E-Cell Members
   - Recruiters

2. Application Layer
   - Dashboards
   - Opportunity Management
   - Role-Based Access Control

3. Trust & Verification Layer
   - Faculty Validation
   - E-Cell Verification
   - Fraud Detection

4. AI Intelligence Layer
   - Recommendation Engine
   - Opportunity Classification
   - Skill Matching

5. Data Layer
   - Student Profiles
   - Opportunity Records
   - Verification Logs
   - Participation Data

---

## Workflow

1. Student creates profile.
2. Opportunity is submitted.
3. AI validates opportunity.
4. Faculty reviews submission.
5. E-Cell performs final verification.
6. Verified opportunity is published.
7. AI recommends opportunities to students.
8. Recruiters search and shortlist candidates.
